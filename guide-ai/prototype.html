FAQ

Questions people ask first.

How long does it take?

Most first-time founders finish in about 12 minutes. Progress saves automatically, so you can leave and resume.

What if the passport scan is wrong?

Every field shows how clearly it was read. Unclear ones are flagged and must be confirmed before they're added. You can also enter details manually.

Can I change something after I finish a section?

Yes. Open any completed section and edit it. Once you submit, the application locks and changes need a formal amendment.

Is my data shared?

Documents are used only to fill this application and are not shared outside the registry.
