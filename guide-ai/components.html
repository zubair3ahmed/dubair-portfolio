Design system

Guide.ai component library

Every token and component here is rendered live from ds.css, the same file the assistant, landing page and case study use. If it looks right here, it looks right in the product.

Colour

One blue for actions and the current step, cool greys for everything else, white cards with a 1px border. Colours are Radix Colors (indigo and slate, steps 1 to 12) and the tokens are also exposed under shadcn/ui's variable names (--background, --primary, --muted, --ring, --radius). Status colours always pair with an icon and text. Click a swatch to copy its value.

Text & status

Contrast

Text-1 passes AAA on white. Text-2 and text-3 are slate 11, about 6:1. White on the brand blue (indigo 9) is about 4.9:1.

Rule

Never show status by colour alone. Pair it with an icon and a word: "Check this", "Acknowledged".

Typography

Lato for Latin text and Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic, so both scripts share one rhythm. Headings are semibold in the darkest grey.

Elevation & motion

Three shadow levels. Motion is short (120–220ms) and switched off for people who ask for reduced motion.

Durations

--sys-motion-duration-fast 120ms for hovers, --sys-motion-duration-base 220ms for entrances, 5s for the AI border rotation.

Reduced motion

All animation drops to near zero, and the AI border stops rotating.

Buttons

One shape: 8px corners, 14px medium text. One primary per view, filled with the brand colour. Secondary buttons sit on a light grey surface, so they read as a lighter choice than primary without needing a border. An outline button is the white option with a grey border.

Surface tokens

Secondary uses light grey (--sys-color-action-secondary-bg) with dark text. Neutral is a lighter grey with a 1px border (--sys-color-action-neutral-bg). Blue tint (--sys-color-brand-subtle) is kept for selected states. All have a hover step and a dark-theme value.

Rule

One primary per view. Use secondary for the second-best action next to it, outline or neutral for cancel and back.

Use

Primary moves the flow forward. Danger is only for irreversible actions, and gets a distinct label ("Submit application", never "OK").

Accessibility

36px minimum height, 44px on touch. Focus shows a 2px blue outline.
