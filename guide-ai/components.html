✦ Design system · Burgundy theme

Guide.ai component library

Every token and component here is rendered live from ds.css, the same file the assistant, landing page and case study use. If it looks right here, it looks right in the product.

Colour

Deep plum leads, a plum-to-violet gradient marks primary actions and AI, and lilac tints carry surfaces. Status colours always pair with an icon and text. Click a swatch to copy its value.

Brand & surfaces

--burgundy · --rose · --rose-tint · --brand · --canvas

Text & status

--text-* · --ok · --warn · --bad
Contrast

Text-1 and text-2 pass AAA on white and lilac. Text-3 passes AA (≥4.5:1). Plum on white is about 12:1.

Rule

Never show status by colour alone. Pair it with an icon and a word: "Check this", "Acknowledged".

Typography

Noto Sans for Latin text and Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic, so both scripts share one rhythm. Headings are bold plum.

Elevation & motion

Three shadow levels. Motion is short (120–220ms) and switched off for people who ask for reduced motion.

Elevation

--e1 · --e2 · --e3
e1 · card
e2 · menu, toast
e3 · modal
Durations

--dur-fast 120ms for hovers, --dur 220ms for entrances, 5s for the AI border rotation.

Reduced motion

All animation drops to near zero, and the AI border stops rotating.
