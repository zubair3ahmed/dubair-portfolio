Density is a feature

Financial users want more data on screen, not less. Progressive disclosure frustrated power users who needed to compare values side by side.

Trust through transparency

The audit trail panel was the most requested feature. In regulated finance, provenance matters as much as the number itself.

Design the exception

Edge cases (partial approvals, criteria amendments, multi-entity splits) were the majority of real usage.
