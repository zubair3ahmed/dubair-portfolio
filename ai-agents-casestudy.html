02 · The Approach

One Chief of Staff, six specialist agents

Think of it like hiring a chief of staff who manages a team. The business owner talks to the Chief of Staff agent in plain language ("launch the new oud collection this Friday") and it breaks the command down, assigns tasks to the right specialist agents, monitors progress, and reports back. Each agent has its own skills, memory, and brand bible baked in.

03 · Architecture

How the system connects

The Chief of Staff sits at the centre. It understands the owner's intent, breaks it into tasks, routes each to the right specialist, and monitors progress. Every agent shares a Brand Bible (voice, visual rules, policies) and has its own skills, tools, and memory.

04 · The Brand Bible

Every agent speaks the same brand language

Before any agent writes a word or makes a decision, it reads from a shared Brand Bible, a living document embedded in every agent's context that ensures consistency across all outputs.

🎨 Visual identity
Colour palette, typography, logo usage, photography style. Every storefront page and marketing asset stays on-brand without manual review.
✍️ Tone of voice
Luxurious but approachable. Never clinical. Product descriptions use sensory language: "warm amber with a whisper of saffron," not "contains amber fragrance compound."
🎯 Target audience
UAE-based professionals, 25–45, who value artisanal quality. Agents tailor messaging to this persona. No generic "dear valued customer" copy.
📜 Policies & SOPs
Return windows, shipping tiers, pricing rules, ingredient disclosures. Agents never contradict business policy because the policies live in their context.

07 · Guardrails

What the agents can't do matters as much as what they can

Every agent has hard boundaries, enforced at the system level, not just prompt instructions the model can ignore.

💰 Spending limits
The Marketing Agent can create ad campaigns but can't set a budget above AED 500/day without owner approval. Hard limit, not a suggestion.
🚫 No health claims
No agent can write that a fragrance is "safe for sensitive skin" or make any medical claim. Ingredient lists only, with "consult your dermatologist" as default.
🔒 Publish requires review
Storefront and Marketing agents draft content but can't publish to the live site or send emails without human approval. Draft → Review → Publish flow.
🤝 Escalation triggers
Support Agent escalates to a human after 3 failed resolution attempts, explicit customer frustration, allergy concerns, or orders above AED 2,000.
🌍 Cultural sensitivity
For UAE/GCC market: no alcohol-based fragrance promos during Ramadan, gender-appropriate marketing when contextually relevant, respectful copy.
📊 Data boundaries
Analytics Agent can read all data but never expose individual customer PII in reports. Aggregated insights only. No customer data leaves the system.

08 · Autonomy Levels

How much freedom each agent gets

Not every task deserves the same autonomy. Low-risk tasks run silently. High-risk ones need the owner in the loop. Match autonomy to consequence.

L0
Fully autonomous
Runs without asking. Low-risk, high-frequency, reversible.
Analytics → daily sales report auto-sent to Slack
L1
Act + notify
Does the action, then tells the owner what it did. Undoable.
Operations → reorders stock at threshold, notifies owner
L2
Draft + approve
Prepares the work, shows a preview, waits for explicit approval.
Marketing → drafts email campaign, owner reviews then sends
L3
Confirm with details
Shows full consequence. Requires deliberate confirmation. High risk.
Storefront → redesigns checkout, shows before/after for approval
L4
Escalate to owner
Out of bounds. Agent can't proceed. Surfaces the situation with full context.
Support → legal threat detected, immediate owner escalation

09 · Onboarding

How I help businesses set this up

This isn't a plug-and-play product. Each brand needs agents configured to its voice, workflows, and risk tolerance. Here's how I work with businesses to design and deploy the full system.

Discovery & Audit

Map the business's current workflows, tools, pain points, and team structure. Identify which tasks are automatable vs. human-critical.

Agent Architecture

Design the master-agent structure: which specialists are needed, what tools they connect to, how they communicate, and the delegation logic.

Knowledge Base Setup

Build each agent's domain knowledge (brand voice docs, product catalog, policies, SOPs, pricing rules) so they speak and act like the brand.

Guardrails & Permissions

Define hard limits, approval flows, escalation triggers, and autonomy levels. What each agent can and can't do, enforced at the system level.

UX & Interface Design

Design the owner's dashboard where they give commands, review drafts, approve actions, and monitor agent activity. The control centre.

Test, Iterate, Launch

Run agents in shadow mode first. Compare agent outputs to human decisions. Tune confidence thresholds. Then go live, one agent at a time.
